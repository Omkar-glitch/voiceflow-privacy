VoiceFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Summary: VoiceFlow keeps a small local history of your transcribed texts on your device so you can re-use them later. Voice recordings are used only to create the transcription and are not retained by VoiceFlow after processing. API keys are stored securely on your device. No data is sent to our servers.

Information We Collect

VoiceFlow stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Transcription history: The last 20 transcribed texts are saved on your device so you can view and re-use them from the History screen. This text is stored only in your device's app storage and is not sent to us.
  • API keys: Your Groq, Gemini, and Sarvam API keys, whichever you configure, are stored securely on your device using the secure storage available on your platform. They are never transmitted to us.
  • App settings: Your language preferences, customization settings, and other in-app choices are saved locally on your device.

Voice recordings are used only to create the transcription and are not retained by VoiceFlow after processing.

How We Use Your Information

Your voice recording is processed depending on the transcription option you choose:

  • Cloud transcription or formatting: Audio and/or transcript text may be sent to the third-party AI service you configure or select.
  • Offline Mode: Voice is processed on your device. Audio and transcript text are not sent to cloud transcription services.

The resulting text is inserted into the active app, copied to the clipboard, or saved to your on-device History depending on your settings. VoiceFlow may block dictation into secure or password fields to help protect sensitive text. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services

Depending on your chosen settings, your audio and/or transcript text may be processed by third-party services such as:

  • Groq: See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini: See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sarvam AI: See Sarvam's Privacy Policy.

These services handle data according to their own privacy policies. If you use Offline Mode, audio and transcript text are not sent to cloud transcription services.

Data Security

  • API keys are stored using secure storage available on your platform.
  • All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Voice recordings are not retained by VoiceFlow after processing.
  • Transcription history is stored only in your device's private app storage.
  • No data is sent to our servers.

Data Retention

VoiceFlow retains the last 20 transcribed texts on your device in the History feature. You can clear this at any time using the Clear History button. Voice recordings are not retained by VoiceFlow after transcription.

Your Rights & Control

  • Clear history: Open the History screen and use "Clear All" to delete all saved transcriptions immediately.
  • Delete API keys: Go to Settings and clear any saved API key at any time.
  • Full deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including history, API keys, and settings, subject to your operating system's normal app data behavior.

Children's Privacy

VoiceFlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not operate any servers, no data from any user, including children, is transmitted to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated through app updates or on this page.
